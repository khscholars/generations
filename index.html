Generations over Dinner

Autumn quarter · Global health

Thursday, November 12, 2026 · Doors 6:00 pm, dinner 6:30 to 8:00 pm · Denning House

Knight-Hennessy Scholars partners with Distinguished Careers Institute Fellows on Generations over Dinner, a global initiative to create meaningful multigenerational experiences and conversations. This quarter, CASBS fellows and Stanford faculty and staff join the table too.

Autumn quarter theme

Global health

Attendees take part in a facilitated dinner conversation on the KHS autumn quarter theme. Tables bring together people from different fields, generations, and parts of the world to talk about health across borders: what shapes it, who it reaches, and how each of us can contribute.
